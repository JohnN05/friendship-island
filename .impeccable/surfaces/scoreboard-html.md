# Scoreboard surface brief

Mode: Experience (projector view), Operate (control view). Single offline file `scoreboard.html`; `#display` hash = projector window (fallback), `#pip` = always-on-top pop-out over the Canva deck, default = control window. Build path: code-led (no image generation).

Audience/job: ballroom audience reads countdown, four scores (0–99) and the leader from the back row; one operator on the same laptop drives everything from a presenter-style control window.

Must carry: Fierce Flamingos, Lucky Lobsters, Daring Dolphins, Snazzy Seals with the deck's animal art; settable timer (3/5/10 presets + custom); leader flag; time's-up moment; two board views (scores / timer) switched from the control view; +1/−1/+3/+10/+7/+4/+2, direct entry, undo + log.

Adaptations (2026-11-04, pop-out over Canva): Venmo QR and CSA logo removed from the board; the deck carries them. The header is a short centred name sign. The timer has its own view: a yellow clock board drops from the sign on ropes while the team cards slide out to the sides, two per side. Team names moved from the deck's Candice script to Josefin Sans on a team-color plate for legibility.

Open: keep or drop the fading "just scored" chalk note (built as fading, 6s).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The score is a hand-painted lifeguard-tower tournament board where wooden number cards hang on hooks; it refuses the glossy game-show panel grid with glowing gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Sun-faded lifeguard red and flag yellow paint over pale weathered plank with visible grain and chips, ocean teal and sand behind, rope and brass hooks, near-black brown ink. Slab sign-painter numerals on cream number cards, chalk-white handwriting for notes.

STORY: The room understands at a glance who leads, by how much, and how long is left; a change lands as a physical event everyone notices.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full 16:9. Short red name sign centred at the top. Scores view: four wide team cards in a 2x2 grid, each a striped badge (animal, name on a team-color plate) beside a yellow panel with two big number cards; the leader's badge flies a red/yellow lifeguard flag. Timer view: a yellow clock board with four big hanging cards (mm:ss) hangs centred from the sign, and the team cards shrink to 50% and slide out to the sides, two per side, picture and score only. No posts or crossbeam.

FORM: Lifeguard Tower Board, position 4 on the ordered list. seed:ff7750caa8d96773. Signature interaction: a changed digit card drops off its hooks and the new card falls on with weight, keeping a one-beat chalk afterglow; fixed digit slots never reflow. Time's up: board shudders, header flashes red/yellow, TIME! placard drops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
